## How to read this

- A model is only interesting if it **beats the covariate/prior baseline** (paired test) and the **permutation null**.
- `<model>+bias` = the same model with the rare-class decision rule (per-class offsets tuned on training donors only). It trades AD/Control accuracy for PD/DLB recall: compare macro-F1 AND per-class recall; AUROC is (almost) unchanged by design.
- Point estimates are computed on seed-averaged probabilities (the same predictions the CI resamples).
- If the leaky-split score is far above the donor-split score, cell-level metrics are inflated by leakage; trust the donor-level numbers.
- PD (~29) and DLB (~33) donors: wide CIs are expected. If a CI includes chance, say so and lean on `lbd_group` or one-vs-control tasks.
- Cohort confound: DLB is 100% MSSM. Compare against the within-cohort permutation null.
